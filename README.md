## 📌 Overview

Wjhny is an AI and AR-based solution for crowd management during Hajj and Umrah. It acts as a smart assistant for pilgrims, combining artificial intelligence with augmented reality to help manage crowds and make the pilgrimage experience safer and smoother.

---

## ❓ The Problem

Every year, millions of pilgrims move through the same holy sites within a short window of time. This creates heavy crowding, which makes movement difficult, raises safety risks, and puts pressure on the teams managing the crowds.

## 💡 Our Solution

Wjhny brings AI and AR together in one assistant. AI supports crowd management, while AR gives pilgrims a visual, easy-to-follow experience on their devices, so guidance reaches them in the moment they need it.
